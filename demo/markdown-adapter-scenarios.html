The MarkdownAdapter (Feature 2, handoffs-review-7.md D1-D15) renders a legacy-JSON-envelope document through renderProducingProcess(…, adapter) and displays the emitted markdown text. The document exercises D3 closed types: headings (h1/h2), paragraph with inline content (strong/em/link with title), blockquote containing a paragraph, a div wrapping a ul with nested ol, a fenced pre block, an hr, and an img.
