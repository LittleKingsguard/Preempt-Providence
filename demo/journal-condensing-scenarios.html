journal-condensing-scenarios — the bounded-journal demo (Feature 3)

The auto-condensing Supervisor journal (Feature 3, docs/specs/handoffs-review-8.md D1-D10): a maxJournalLength init bounds a long-lived host's journal growth. A state-slice stream exceeds the threshold → the deferred microtask condense rewrites the pre-base entries into ONE base marker (a serialized graph snapshot). A replay() then restores the graph (graph-REPLACE) + re-applies the post-base entries; a post-base undo() operates on the restored graph (D3 id-resolution). The banner is journal-condensing-scenarios; the profile line is [journal-condensing:profile].
